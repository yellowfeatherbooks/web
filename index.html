Arundhati Roy's
Mother Mary — A Kerala Story the World is Reading

Arundhati Roy's memoir Mother Mary Comes to Me — named NYT's Best Books of 2025 and winner of the Mathrubhumi Book of the Year 2026 — is a searing account of the author's childhood in Kerala, her complex bond with her mother Mary Roy, and the making of The God of Small Things. With reviews in the Guardian, New Yorker, Financial Times and more, this Kerala-rooted book is the most discussed Indian literary work of the season. Meanwhile the International Booker 2026 shortlist drops on March 31, with 13 global novels jostling for the world's most influential translated fiction prize.

Published just today on Manorama Online's literature portal, Gopan Ambatt's new story paints an intimate Kerala moment — a scooter resting in the shade of a tree, a sudden pang of hunger, and the weight of an unspoken afternoon.
